# Running a Games Tournament

## A simple 30-minute classroom format

1. Choose two or three games that match your learners' level.
2. Put students in pairs or small teams.
3. Give each team five minutes to practise the controls.
4. Run three short rounds and record each result.
5. Award points for accuracy, teamwork, and respectful turn-taking.

## Suggested schedule

- 0-5 minutes: explain the goal and model one round.
- 5-20 minutes: play three timed rounds.
- 20-25 minutes: final challenge round.
- 25-30 minutes: celebrate progress and reflect on strategies.

## Teacher checklist

- Test every game before class.
- Pair students with a clear role for reading, clicking, or coaching.
- Keep a visible score sheet.
- Praise improvement, not only winning.
- Offer a quiet alternative for students who need a break.
