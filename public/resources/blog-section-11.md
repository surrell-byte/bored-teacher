## Why This Section Matters

Teaching is deeply human work. You're not just teaching a language—you're influencing how students see themselves, what they believe they can achieve, and their relationship with learning itself. This section focuses on the people skills, emotional intelligence, and self-care that make teaching sustainable and rewarding.
