# Flags of the World - Reference Card

Use this card as a quick pre-game reference for Flagmaster.

## Visual clues

- Stripes: count the bands and note whether they are horizontal or vertical.
- Stars: check the number, colour, and position.
- Crosses: look for a central cross or a corner cross.
- Emblems: identify shields, coats of arms, suns, birds, or plants.
- Colours: use colour combinations as clues, not as the only evidence.

## Study routine

1. Look at the flag for five seconds.
2. Say one visual clue aloud.
3. Connect the clue to a country or region.
4. Check the answer and explain your reasoning.
