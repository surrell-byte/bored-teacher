## Game background clarity

Render game background artwork at full clarity. Do not place translucent white overlays, `filter: blur()`, or `backdrop-filter` on a game background or its background image layer unless the user specifically requests that treatment. Keep intentional translucent surfaces and blur on foreground interface panels, separate from the background artwork.
